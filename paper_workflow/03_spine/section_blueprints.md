# Section blueprints

## Abstract

Operational decision gap → raw-data renewal cost → pointwise regret labels → experiment-held-out RGB versus time control → bounded implication and retrospective limitation.

## Introduction

1. Defrost timing is an economic decision under progressive frost accumulation.
2. Existing optimal-timing and image-control studies leave the per-frame supervision problem unresolved.
3. A single timestamp and frame-random validation are scientifically inadequate.
4. Present the cost-regret-to-image bridge and its shortcut-controlled evaluation.

## Results

1. Unsmoothed data yield auditable policy-conditional cost surfaces.
2. Broad or disconnected low-regret sets motivate pointwise image states.
3. Five-model development selects the minimal high-confidence binary baseline.
4. Full-cohort leave-one-experiment-out results compare RGB, time and RGB+time.
5. Per-experiment and regret-weighted failures bound generalization.

## Discussion

Interpret the methodological bridge; relate it to optimal-defrost and image-control precedents; explain visual increment versus temporal shortcut; discuss fixed-ticket, offline-anchor, observational and comfort-measurement limits; specify the prospective intervention needed for control claims.

## Methods

Cohort and raw integration; clean reference and thermal-service proxy; observed ticket reconstruction; renewal-cost candidates and regret; timestamp join and experiment split; image sampling/features/five-model development; locked RBF-SVM and camera groups; LOEO evaluation, bootstrap and misclassification regret; data/code availability.
