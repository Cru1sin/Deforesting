# Confirmed motivation

## Controlling motivation

Historical heat-pump operation provides neither counterfactual early-defrost outcomes nor a decision label for each frost image. This study therefore constructs an explicit, policy-conditional empirical cost surface from unsmoothed measurements, retains candidate-level regret as timing uncertainty, and asks whether RGB frost appearance predicts high-confidence pre/post-optimal states across unseen experiments beyond a matched time-only control.

## Confirmation status

Confirmed by the author's repeated instruction to implement the complete cost-function → optimal-point → image-classification Spark idea as the paper's central methodology. Option A is selected; Option B is retained as the ambiguity mechanism inside it, and Option C is retained as the cost-stage foundation rather than the whole paper.

## Rejected framings

- “First optimal defrost method”: contradicted by direct prior work.
- “First image-based defrost method”: contradicted by direct image-control work.
- “Nature-level realized savings and comfort improvement”: unsupported without prospective interventions and direct indoor measurements.
- “A precise true optimum for every cycle”: contradicted by broad and sometimes disconnected low-regret sets.

## Scope limits

All reported optima are empirical and conditional on the observed fixed-duration defrost ticket and reference assumptions. RGB evidence is retrospective and associational. Time-progress features are diagnostic controls because their normalized endpoint is known only after the observed cycle ends. The already inspected historical test subset is not described as blind confirmation.
