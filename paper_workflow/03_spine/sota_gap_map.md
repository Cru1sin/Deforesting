## Gap Summary

- The defensible novelty is not a new defrost optimum or the first use of images. It is an auditable translation from cycle-level, policy-conditional cost regret to frame-level RGB labels that retains uncertainty around broad or disconnected minima.
- The core validation gap is experimental isolation: all frames from one experiment must remain together, and claims must be supported by experiment-level rather than frame-level uncertainty.
- The decisive empirical test is incremental value over a matched time-only control. Until the full-cohort comparison and intervals are complete, RGB decision value remains provisional and no energy-saving or deployment benefit should be claimed.
