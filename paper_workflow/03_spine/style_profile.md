# Style profile

| Style dimension | Target venue expectation | Exemplar pattern | Applied to this paper |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening | Broad relevance followed quickly by a concrete unresolved problem | Name the operational decision before the sensing method | Open with when to defrost, then expose the missing per-frame decision label |
| Contribution | One technically convincing advance with cross-field meaning | Optimization-to-action and sensing-to-control bridges | Cost regret to RGB supervision, not a new optimizer or camera model |
| Results | Claim-first, evidence-led progression | Objective, validation, benchmark, robustness, boundary | Cost surface, regret labels, time control, held-out heterogeneity |
| Statistics | Independent units and transparent uncertainty | Group repeated images and report coverage/risk | Experiment-held-out estimates and experiment-level bootstrap intervals |
| Language | Concrete, bounded and reproducible | Operational verbs and explicit scope | Use policy-conditional, empirical, retrospective and associational |
| Ending | Implication plus a precise limitation | Withhold claims beyond the experiment | Prospective controlled defrost timing is the next test, not a reported result |
